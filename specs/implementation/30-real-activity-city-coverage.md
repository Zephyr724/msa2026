# Slice 30 — Real Activity City Coverage and Credited Photography

## Status

**Accepted — explicitly requested by the product owner on 2026-08-05.**

## Human request interpretation

Extend the existing ten-item real New Zealand assessment catalogue by another
twenty activities, rather than stopping at twenty total. The final catalogue
therefore contains thirty activities. Add several useful choices in major
cities, including Wellington, and use relevant free-to-use photography from
Pexels or an equivalent traceable source.

## Scope and boundaries

- Preserve the ten accepted Slice 29 activities and their existing
  project-owned SVG covers.
- Insert twenty new Admin-curated external Quests without changing the schema,
  dependency set, authentication model, or authorization model.
- Add four country-level administrative regions needed for city filtering:
  Hamilton City, Dunedin City, Nelson City, and Palmerston North City.
- Use ongoing, recurring, self-paced, or provider-directory activities so the
  catalogue does not depend mainly on short-lived event dates.
- Keep the provider page authoritative. Do not infer attendance, availability,
  ecological outcomes, or an exact event date from a directory listing.
- Use Pexels stock photos only as relevant illustrations. Never describe them
  as photographs of the named New Zealand programme or its participants.

  legacy upgrade. Operator edits are not overwritten.
- The twenty new deterministic Quest and image IDs are inserted only when
  absent and remain stable across repeated startup.
- The four new region IDs fail closed on a name, type, or parent collision.
- Existing fictional reviewer completion history is unchanged and continues to
  use only the accepted Slice 29 history subset.

## Acceptance criteria

- The assessment catalogue contains exactly thirty deterministic Quests and
  thirty cover images after one or repeated seed runs.
- Exactly twenty covers have an HTTPS `images.pexels.com` image URL, a Pexels
  photo-page source URL, a non-empty creator name, and the accepted licence and
  illustrative-use note.
- The final catalogue exposes multiple activities for Auckland, Wellington,
  Christchurch, Hamilton, Tauranga, and Dunedin, plus coverage for Nelson and
  Palmerston North.
- Official activity sources and image sources use HTTPS.
- Existing operator edits and assessment-account history remain unchanged.
- Backend build, unit tests, and PostgreSQL integration tests pass.
- No schema, dependency, authentication, authorization, deployment, credential,
  staging, commit, push, or pull-request change is included.
