## Reconstructed implementation instruction

Extend the assessment environment with twenty natural-feeling but explicitly
fictional Community posts. Use the existing assessment identities and real
provider-backed Quests. Demonstrate the product value of posts through
reflection, practical tips, questions, encouragement, Related Quest discovery,
images, tags, likes, root comments, and direct replies.

Reuse only the already checked, credited Pexels Quest-cover set. Do not imply
that stock photography documents the named activity, and do not represent the
stories as real participation or evidence. Put a direct fictional-showcase
disclosure in every title and body so a feed visitor does not need to inspect a
tag or implementation document to understand the fixture. Keep the fixture
deterministic, idempotent, insert-only for existing posts, transactionally tied
to the assessment-account bootstrap, and covered by PostgreSQL integration
tests.

Do not change the schema, dependencies, authentication, authorization, API
contract, or frontend. Do not stage, commit, push, deploy, or create a pull
request.
